Predatory Crimes Unit Aguilar, Fermin E Mon, 09/14/2026 - 14:26 Who We Are The Bureau of Indian Affairs (BIA) Predatory Crimes Unit is dedicated to protecting Tribal communities from some of the most serious and damaging offenses facing Indian Country today. Our investigators specialize in crimes that target the vulnerable, exploit trust, and often cross jurisdictional lines — working alongside Tribal, federal, state, and local partners to bring offenders to justice and support survivors. We understand that these crimes carry a heavy toll on individuals, families, and communities.
