Inocente Richmond Yanez Himel, Rikki L Mon, 08/17/2026 - 17:54 Inocente Richmond Yanez “Shawn” went missing on January 5, 1999 and was last known to be in Tahlequah, OK. He is described as having brown hair, brown eyes, 6' 2" in height, and 180 lbs in weight. The Bureau of Indian Affairs Missing and Murdered Unit and the Tahlequah Police Department are seeking information that may assist them with the ongoing search.
