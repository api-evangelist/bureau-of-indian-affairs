Navigation Efficiency Instead of targeting a specific number of clicks to reach information, focus on the effort required to complete a task Aguilar, Fermin E Wed, 07/15/2026 - 15:36 Focus on Effort, Not Clicks Design for Task Completion: Ensure the user's path is logical and predictable. A user might be willing to click five times if each step provides clear progress and relevant information, whereas a single click to a confusing page can lead to frustration. Use Descriptive Labels: Help users navigate by using clear, action-oriented labels on links and buttons.
